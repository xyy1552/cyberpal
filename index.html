HARDWARE MODE GUIDE

SAFE OPS ONLY

iPhone 7 / 7 Plus (A10) — Enter DFU

  1. Connect iPhone to Android via USB OTG cable.
  2. Hold Side + Volume Down for 8–10 seconds until screen goes black.
  3. Release Side, keep holding Volume Down for 5 seconds.
  4. Screen remains black; device enumerates as 05AC:1227.

iPhone 8 / X and Newer — Enter DFU

  1. Quick-press Volume Up, quick-press Volume Down.
  2. Hold Side until screen goes black, then hold Side + Volume Down for 5 seconds.
  3. Release Side, continue holding Volume Down for 5–10 seconds.
